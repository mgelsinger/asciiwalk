          THE WORLD BEHIND THE WORDS
          
Real, with room for imagination.

        

        
      

      

        Streets and building outlines come from geographic data. Ground contours follow USGS elevation.
        Building heights combine mapped data and estimates.
      

      

        Warsaw's downtown includes architectural details interpreted from dated photographs. Trees, people,
        and traffic bring a little imagined life to the scene. This is a place to explore, not a live view or
        navigation service.
      

      

      

        Only printable ASCII characters draw the world. Original photographs are not displayed in the scene.
